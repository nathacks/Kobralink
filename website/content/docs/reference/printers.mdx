Kobralink supports the **Kobra X** and the **Kobra 4**.

| Model | Build volume (mm) | Nozzles (mm) | Max nozzle / bed | ACE |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kobra X | 260 × 260 × 260 | **0.4**, 0.25, 0.6, 0.8 | 300 °C / 100 °C | Built in |
| Kobra 4 | 260 × 260 × 260 | **0.4**, 0.25, 0.6, 0.8 | 300 °C / 100 °C | Optional |

The standard nozzle is in bold. These are the specifications published by Anycubic; they are used by the
[queue](/en/docs/guide/queue#compatibility).

## Other models

Kobralink also knows the specifications of the Kobra 3, Kobra 3 V2, Kobra 3 Max, Kobra S1 and Kobra S1 Max, which use a related LAN
protocol. They are **not supported or tested**.
