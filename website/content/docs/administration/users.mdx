title: Users and roles
description: Accounts and what each role can do.
---

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Administrator | Everything |
| Operator | Control printers, change settings, create API keys |
| Viewer | Read only |

Only administrators can manage users, turn the MCP server on or off, change its tool permissions, download or restore a
backup, and restart Kobralink.

A viewer can still turn on push notifications on their own device and disconnect the AI apps they authorized.

## Manage accounts

The first account is created by the setup wizard and is an administrator. Sign-up is then closed. Administrators create the
other accounts in **Settings → Users**, with a password of at least 8 characters, and can change a role or a password there.

- At least one administrator must remain.
- You cannot remove your own administrator role or delete your own account.
- Deleting a user closes their sessions immediately.

Sessions last 30 days and are renewed while in use.

<Callout type="info">Only give accounts to people you trust, including read-only ones.</Callout>
