Every G-code sent to Kobralink, from OrcaSlicer or from the browser, is kept in **one library for every printer**. Only
`.gcode` and `.bgcode` files are accepted.

## Tabs

| Tab | Content |
| --- | --- |
| All | Every stored file |
| Web | Files uploaded from the browser and not yet verified |
| Printer | Files in the printer's internal storage |
| History | Past prints |
| Timelapses | Recorded videos |

## Add files

Drop one or more files on the card, or click to browse. Files can also come from OrcaSlicer or from any
Moonraker client.

A file uploaded from the browser was not sliced for this printer by OrcaSlicer, so Kobralink asks you to confirm before
printing it. You can turn this off with **Warn on web uploads** in the
[printer settings](/en/docs/administration/printer-settings#printing).

## File cards

Each card shows the thumbnail, estimated time, filaments with the amount each colour needs, last print and the
[estimated cost](/en/docs/guide/statistics#costs).

**Preview** opens the part in 3D, layer by layer. During a print, *Follow* keeps the view on the current layer and shows the nozzle at
its real position, read from the printer every second. The print head matches your machine (Kobra X, or Kobra 4 for the others);
the **Show print head** button hides or shows it again. It mirrors the printer live: the tubes on top take the colour of the loaded
filaments, the fan spins at its real speed and the Kobra X screen shows the number of the channel in use.
The part takes the colours of the slots actually used: the ones picked when the print was started from Kobralink, otherwise
the ones the nozzle prints as the print goes. A drop of the loaded filament's colour comes out of the nozzle. Previews
are not available for `.bgcode` files. The plate is the printer's build plate at its real size; the menu at the top left switches between the **Official Anycubic** sheet, a plain **Textured PEI** sheet and **None (grid)**, and the choice is saved for each printer: the part sits where it was sliced, and the print head can leave it, as on the machine when it probes or parks.

## Printer storage

The **Printer** tab lists the files stored on the printer itself. You can print or delete them, and see the preview made
by the slicer. Deleting a file here does not remove it from Kobralink.

- The printer lists only its newest files (256 at most on the Kobra 4).
- *Test models* are the sample files that came with the printer. They cannot be deleted.

To free the storage automatically, turn on **Delete from printer after print** in the printer settings.
