## Printers page

The **Printers** page shows a card for each printer, with its state, progress, remaining time and finish time. Counters at the
top filter the list: printing, paused, ready, bed to clear, maintenance, error and offline. The page also shows which
printer finishes next.

On a computer, the navigation is a sidebar that folds into a rail of icons. On a phone, it is a bar at the bottom.

## Printer page

| Card | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Print | Current job: progress, layer, elapsed and remaining time, 3D view, pause, resume, cancel, skip objects |
| Temperatures | Live chart, nozzle and bed targets, presets |
| Camera | Live stream and full screen, see [Camera](/en/docs/guide/camera) |
| Controls | Speed, part fan, light, smart plug, axes |
| Filaments | Slots, loading, drying, see [Filament](/en/docs/guide/filament) |
| Print queue | Items this printer can take, see [Queue](/en/docs/guide/queue) |
| Files | Library, printer storage, timelapses, see [Files](/en/docs/guide/files) |
| Macros | One-click actions, see [Macros](/en/docs/guide/macros) |
| Maintenance | Tasks and due dates, see [Maintenance](/en/docs/guide/maintenance) |

## Controls

**Temperatures.** The nozzle accepts 0–320 °C and the bed 0–120 °C; your printer may allow less. The presets are:

| Preset | Nozzle | Bed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLA | 210 °C | 60 °C |
| PETG | 235 °C | 75 °C |
| ABS | 250 °C | 95 °C |
| Off | 0 °C | 0 °C |

**Speed.** Three modes: Calm, Standard and Sport. They are the only speeds the printer offers over LAN, and they apply to
the current print.

**Axes.** Move X, Y and Z by 1, 10 or 50 mm, home XYZ, XY or Z, and turn the motors off. Locked while printing.

**Light and fan.** Switch the camera light and set the part fan.

**Power.** Available once a [smart plug](/en/docs/integrations/home-assistant#smart-plug-and-energy) is chosen.

## Cancelling a print

Cancelling asks for an optional reason (spaghetti, adhesion, clog, quality…). The reason feeds the failure statistics. A
cancelled print cannot be resumed.

## Appearance

Theme (system, light or dark) and interface language are in **Settings → General**. They are kept in the browser.

## Read-only accounts

A viewer sees the same pages with the controls disabled. See [Users and roles](/en/docs/administration/users).
