## History

The **History** page lists every print with its preview, duration, filament, energy and cost. For a failed print you can:

- choose a reason: spaghetti, bed adhesion, clog, filament runout, layer shift, power loss, cancelled on purpose,
  rejected quality, other
- add a note
- see the camera snapshot taken at the end, if the camera was running
- read the error code reported by the printer

## Statistics

Choose a printer and a period: 7, 30, 90 or 365 days, or everything. The page shows:

- prints, success rate, print time and filament, compared with the previous period
- prints, hours and cost per day or month, by weekday and by start hour
- use by material and the most printed files
- for each printer: utilisation, mean time between failures, idle time after a print, accuracy of the slicer estimate,
  measured energy
- failures by reason, most frequent first
- filament wasted by prints that never finished

**Export CSV** downloads the prints of the selection.

## Costs

Cost = filament + energy.

**Filament.** The price comes from the Spoolman spool. Without one, the *default filament price* applies
(**Settings → General**). Filament without any price is shown as *unpriced*.

**Energy.** Measured by the plug's meter when [Home Assistant](/en/docs/integrations/home-assistant#smart-plug-and-energy) is
set up. Otherwise estimated:

```text
average power (W) ÷ 1000 × hours × price per kWh
```

The average power is the printer's own, from its profile, or the default in **Settings → General**. Leave the electricity
price at 0 to ignore energy.

An estimated cost is shown before printing on the file card, in the preview and in the print dialog. The actual cost is computed at the end.
