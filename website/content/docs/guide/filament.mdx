## Slots

The **Filaments** card shows what the printer reports. Three wirings are detected automatically:

| Mode | Slots |
| --- | --- |
| Toolhead (buffer) | The slots of the toolhead |
| Direct ACE | Four slots per ACE unit |
| Toolhead + ACE (hub) | Three toolhead slots, plus the slots of the ACE units plugged into the fourth |

Without an ACE, choose the **external spool** loaded on the printer's own holder; its usage is deducted from it.

Click a slot to set its material and colour (written to the printer's screen), its OrcaSlicer profile and its spool, and
to load or unload the filament. Filament read from an RFID tag is recognised.

## Drying

Start a cycle from the ACE section of the card: 30–80 °C for 10 to 1440 minutes. Your ACE may refuse the highest temperatures.
You can also schedule a cycle for later.

Presets are edited in the **Filament** page. The defaults are:

| Preset | Temperature | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLA, PLA+ | 45 °C | 4 h |
| PETG | 50 °C | 4 h |
| TPU | 55 °C | 4 h |
| ABS / ASA | 55 °C | 8 h |
| PA / PC | 55 °C | 12 h |

**Auto feed** turns the automatic refill of an ACE unit on or off.

## OrcaSlicer profiles

Each slot can carry an OrcaSlicer profile, sent to OrcaSlicer as brand and preset name when it syncs filaments. Without a choice,
Kobralink picks one from the material.

To add your own profiles, open **Filament** and import a ZIP or `.json` files from OrcaSlicer. They are marked with a ★. To
shorten the list, choose the visible brands in the printer settings.

## Spools

Two ways to track filament:

| | Spoolman | Local inventory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where spools live | In [Spoolman](/en/docs/integrations/spoolman) | In Kobralink |
| Prices | From Spoolman | Default price per kg |
| Use it when | You want one source shared with other tools | You do not run Spoolman |

The local inventory is used as long as no Spoolman URL is set. Add spools with their material, colour and initial net
weight; the remaining weight is estimated from the usage the printer reports.

Assign a spool to a slot in the slot dialog. From then on, its usage is deducted while printing, the print dialog warns
when it is too short, and the queue takes it into account.

A low-spool alert is sent below the threshold set in the printer settings (*Spool almost empty*, off by default).
